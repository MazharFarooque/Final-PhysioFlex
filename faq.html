Frequenty Asked Questions

01

What conditions do you treat at PhysioFlex Clinic?

We treat a wide range of conditions including back pain, neck pain, slip disc, sciatica, sports injuries, post-surgical rehabilitation, neurological conditions, arthritis, and posture-related problems. Each treatment plan is customized based on the patient’s condition.

02

How many physiotherapy sessions will I need?

The number of sessions depends on your condition, severity, and recovery goals. Acute pain may require 5–8 sessions, while chronic or post-surgical cases may need 10–30 sessions. Your therapist will guide you after assessment.

03

Do you provide home visit physiotherapy services?

Yes, we offer home visit physiotherapy for elderly patients, post-operative recovery, neurological cases, and individuals with limited mobility. Treatment is delivered safely and professionally at your convenience.

04

Is physiotherapy painful?

Physiotherapy is generally safe and controlled. Some mild discomfort may occur during exercises or mobilization techniques, but it is part of the healing process. Our therapists ensure treatment remains within safe limits.

05

Do I need a doctor’s referral to start physiotherapy?

No referral is required in most cases. You can directly book an appointment with us. However, if you have recent surgery or medical conditions, bringing your medical reports is helpful for proper assessment.

06

How long does each physiotherapy session last?

Each session typically lasts 40–60 minutes depending on the treatment plan and condition. Sessions include assessment, therapy techniques, and guided exercises.
